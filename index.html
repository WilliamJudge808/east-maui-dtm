Maui at One Meter

Airborne lidar maps the ground beneath a forest canopy at about a meter. NOAA and USGS flew it over Maui County from 2020 to 2023, but persistent low cloud kept the aircraft out of windward East Maui. That left about 490 km² of steep rainforest with nothing finer than 30 m satellite elevation data.

This map fills the gap. A neural network reads aerial photographs, satellite imagery and the 30 m elevation model together, and predicts the shape of the ground under the trees. Where lidar exists, the map shows the lidar, and the dashed line marks where it ends.
